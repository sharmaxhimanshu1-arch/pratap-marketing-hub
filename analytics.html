How the weekly numbers get here
  1. Every Monday, export Content (30 days), Followers (365 days) and Visitors for each page and drop them in the week's folder on Drive.
  2. Ask Claude to refresh the hub, then unlock this page with the password.
  3. To check one file yourself: open the page on LinkedIn as admin, go to Analytics, use Export, then pick the company above and add the file.

Menu names on LinkedIn change now and then. If a step looks different, look for the Export button in Analytics.

What the words mean
Views (impressions)
How many times a post appeared on someone's screen. One person can count more than once.
Actions
Reactions, comments, shares and link clicks added together. They show how many views led to something.
Per 100 views
A fairer way to compare pages. If the number is 4, then out of every 100 times a post was shown, about 4 led to an action.
Second half vs first half
The period is split in the middle. An up arrow means the second half did better than the first. It is a quick way to see direction, not a forecast.
New followers
People who started following the page during the period. It does not include people who left.

Pages are compared with each other and with their own earlier weeks. They are not compared with other companies, because those figures need a proper source.

How to add your numbers
  1. Open a company page on LinkedIn as admin and go to its Analytics.
  2. Choose Content, pick a date range (30 days works well), and use Export. Do the same under Followers if you want follower numbers.
  3. Pick the company below and drop the downloaded file in. Repeat for each page.

This works until LinkedIn approves API access, after which the numbers can arrive on their own. The files are read inside your browser. They are not uploaded anywhere or saved on the site. Menu names on LinkedIn change now and then, so if a step looks different, look for the Export button in Analytics.

What the words mean
Views (impressions)
How many times a post appeared on someone's screen. One person can count more than once.
Reactions, comments, shares, clicks
Each is a small action someone took on a post. Added together, they show how many views led to something.
Per 100 views
A fairer way to compare. If the number is 4, then out of every 100 times the post was shown, about 4 led to an action.
Second half vs first half
The period is split in the middle. If views are up, the second half did better than the first. It is a quick way to see direction.
New followers
People who started following the page during the period. It does not include people who left.

The page compares your pages with each other and with their own earlier weeks. It does not compare with other companies, because those figures need a proper source.
